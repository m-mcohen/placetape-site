Placetape

Placetape is a private Android app that reminds you of things when you arrive at a place or leave it.

What it does

  • You save places, such as Home or a store, and make a reminder for when you arrive there or leave.
  • When you arrive or leave, your phone shows the reminder, even with the app closed. Phone settings and a weak location fix can delay one.
  • If you choose to sign in with Google, Placetape also reads location reminders you add to Google Tasks, for example by voice with Gemini, and writes the place into that task's details. Signing in is optional; the app works without it.

Who it is for

Placetape is shared privately with a small group of people. It is not sold and is not in the Play Store.
